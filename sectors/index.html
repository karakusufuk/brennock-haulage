Food & Cold Chain

Chilled and frozen, from producer to distribution centre, with the temperature logged every mile.

What we carry

Chilled and frozen food on pallets: dairy, meat, ready meals, fresh produce and bakery.

How we carry it

Refrigerated 13.6m trailers, single or dual temperature from −25°C to +8°C. Every load is pre-cooled, sealed and logged, with the record sent to you on delivery.

Certified for

  • ATP certified fleet
  • HACCP
  • BRCGS Storage & Distribution

Construction Materials

Steel, board and block to site, booked to the crane, not to our timetable.

What we carry

Rebar, plasterboard, blocks, timber, insulation and roofing on pallets or in bundles.

How we carry it

Curtainsiders and flatbeds with ratchet straps, edge protectors and timber bearers. We call ahead so the site is ready for the drop.
