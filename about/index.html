Built for the long road.

A family yard in Ashbourne, moving Ireland’s freight since 2004.

Brennock started with one truck and one customer, a dairy in Meath that needed its milk in Dublin before six. That run still goes out every morning.

Today we run ten trucks out of the same yard. We are big enough to cover the whole country and small enough that you talk to the person planning your load, not a call centre.

Ten trucks, one yard.

Every truck starts and ends its day here. Reefers, curtainsiders and a flatbed, all on our own plates, all serviced in our own workshop. Nothing is subcontracted to a name you have never heard of.

Checked before it leaves.

Every driver walks round their truck before the first mile: lights, tyres, seals. Reefers are pre-cooled before the first pallet goes in. It takes ten minutes and it is the reason our loads arrive the way they left.

Loaded right the first time.

Pallets go in to the plan and every strap is tensioned and checked twice. If something is wrong at loading, we fix it at loading, not at your dock.

Tell us what needs moving.

One load or every week, we will come back to you with a price the same day.
